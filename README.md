# 2D RPG

My first game, made in Godot 4. You move around a 2D world and fight slimes.

**[▶ Play in your browser](https://nicolaschung.itch.io/godot-rpg)**

![Gameplay](https://github.com/nic242/godot-rpg/blob/main/combat.gif)

## Features
- Enemy chase AI and melee combat
- Health bars that shift colours
- Damage and regen popups, with regen only out of combat
- 8-direction movement
- Scene transitions between the world and the cliffside

## Controls
- WASD: move
- (SPACE): attack
